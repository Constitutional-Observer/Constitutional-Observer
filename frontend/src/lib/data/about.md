The nation is constituted by more than the singular document that we call the Constitution. Everyday, there are different distinct actions performed that make the nation imagine and work towards the future. These actions include debate happening in legislative houses in the states and the union, decisions taken by government(s), and the citizen's direct involvement in imagining specific actions

Every aspect of this soil is touched by decisions taken across this wide-range of governments. Every being, human, plant, insect or otherwise is affected. 

The Constitutional Observer intends to collect these deliberations, debates and decisions that constitute the country in specific ways. It is an excercise in expanding the understanding of how the constitution of the country is always in progress. 

## The Collection

The Observer presently collects from 
- Debates in the Constituent Assembly
- Debates in the Lok Sabha 
- Debates in the Rajya Sabha
- Debates from 10 State Legislatures (Assembly and Council)
- Environment Impact Assessment Minutes of Meetings
